About Me

I'm a robotics software enthusiast currently pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at IIITDM Kancheepuram. In my second year, I'm deeply involved in hands-on robotics development through competitive team activities and academic research.

My core interests lie in robotic manipulation, perception pipelines, motion planning, and real-time system integration using ROS2. I have practical experience with LiDAR and depth cameras, implementing Cartesian control via MoveIt2 Servo, and developing software tightly coupled with hardware systems such as the Jetson AGX Orin.

I believe great robotics engineering lives at the boundary between clean abstraction and messy hardware — and I enjoy operating in exactly that space.
